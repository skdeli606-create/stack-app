Undo Stack

A text editor whose Undo and Redo are powered by two stacks (LIFO). Every change is pushed on; undo pops the most recent one first.

Editor 0 characters

Shortcuts: Ctrl/Cmd+Z to undo, Ctrl+Y or Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z to redo. Typing is saved as one step after a short pause.
